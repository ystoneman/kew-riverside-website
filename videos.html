Kew Riverside Parent Voices

Some things are best
said in your own voice.

What does Kew Riverside mean to you? Send a short video about your experience, what you value, or what you want people to understand.

There is no script to follow. A 30–90 second phone recording is plenty.

Your video.
Your choice to share.

Submit a video for publication on YouTube. New submissions require your explicit YouTube permission. News-media permission is optional. First save your permissions, then upload privately to Dropbox; nothing is published automatically.

A video is not an official council response. For this initial consultation, open the council form (stated deadline: 16 October 2026).

Prefer to speak privately? Contact Yann privately without submitting a video.

Start your video submission ↗︎

No Google or Dropbox sign-in required
Step 1: permissions in Google Forms.
Step 2: follow its confirmation link to Dropbox.
Adults recording themselves only. Aim for under 100 MB.

Already saved your permissions? Continue to Dropbox upload. Use the same email in both steps. One video per permission form. Your saved permissions still apply, including any earlier private-only choice, even if you upload later.

Read the video privacy notice

A place to start

Pick one thought.
Make it yours.

  1. “We chose Kew Riverside because…”
  2. “One moment that shows what this school means to us…”
  3. “What has mixed-age learning been like for your child?”
  4. “What I would like the council to understand is…”

What happens after you send it?

  1. 01

    It arrives privately.

    Your video goes to Yann’s private Dropbox; your permissions stay in Google Forms. Other contributors cannot see them. Yann matches both using your email, upload details and any filename you provide.

  2. 02

    Yann watches it.

    He checks the matching permissions, reviews the video personally and may contact you about edits. An unmatched upload stays private. Nothing is published automatically.

  3. 03

    You choose what can be shared.

    For new submissions, your required YouTube permission lets Yann post it on Kew Riverside Parent Voices on YouTube. Publication is not guaranteed. A separate, optional choice lets Yann supply your video and chosen credit to news organisations for their coverage of Kew Riverside’s future. Leaving it unchecked does not prevent submission.

A few simple recording tips

Find a quiet spot, face a window and hold your phone steady at eye level. Portrait or landscape is fine. Speak naturally; no editing, music or special equipment needed.

Record only yourself, with no children or other identifiable people in the picture or background audio. Leave out children’s names, health or SEND details, private records and allegations about identifiable people. Check the background for addresses, documents and photographs.

Use your own recording and material you have permission to share. All viewpoints are welcome; threats, abuse, impersonation and spam are not.

Your name, permission and removal

Enter the same email address in the permission form and Dropbox. The filename question is optional if your phone does not show it. Your email, filename and the name entered at upload stay private. The form has a separate, optional public credit: a first name or nickname is fine, or leave it blank. Your face and voice can still identify you.

New submissions require YouTube publication permission. Videos can share parent experiences, raise awareness and help prospective families learn about the school. You permit trimming pauses and adding accurate captions without changing your meaning; other substantive edits need your agreement. Earlier saved permissions keep their original scope.

News-media permission is optional and unchecked. If you select it, Yann may supply your video and chosen public credit to news organisations reporting on Kew Riverside’s future, for use of the recording and excerpts in broadcast and online coverage, with accurate captions and edits that preserve your meaning. Your email and other private contact details are not shared. News organisations control their own publications and may need their own release or privacy arrangements.

You can withdraw permission, change your credit or ask for removal. You can withdraw YouTube or news-media permission separately. Yann can remove his YouTube upload, stop further media sharing and contact known recipients about your request, but cannot guarantee recall of broadcasts or copies made by others. Read the full notice.

Trouble uploading, or using an older link?

No account is needed for the two-step route above. The permission confirmation is not an upload receipt: follow its Dropbox link, choose your video and wait for Dropbox to confirm the upload. Use the same email in both steps. Keep a 30–90 second recording under 100 MB where possible; try a shorter clip or a reliable connection if the upload fails.

The original Google video form is still open if you already have that link or prefer it. It requires Google sign-in and accepts one video up to 1 GB, with one response per account. New responses use the same required YouTube and optional news-media choices. Earlier recorded choices remain valid. A complete submission there already includes its own permissions; you do not need to submit it again through Dropbox.

If either service is full or unavailable, or you want to replace a video, contact Yann privately to arrange an alternative. Please do not put a private video link in the public ideas board.

You can also write a community letter without an account.
