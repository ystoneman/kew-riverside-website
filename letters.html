Not sure how to begin? Tap a starter:

Not sure how to begin? Try “We chose Kew Riverside because…” or “One moment that shows what this school means to us…”.

What could I write about?
  • What the school means to your child and family, in your own words.
  • What closure would change for you: the journey, cost, siblings, routines or friendships.
  • Anything you think the council has missed.
  • What you are asking the council to do.

Leave out sensitive details about children or others (a first name alone is fine), health or SEND information, confidential records, private contact details and allegations about identifiable people. No threats, abuse, spam or impersonation. Your own name and postcode for the council go in the private fields in step 2, not here. Letter guidelines.

A letter is your opinion.

  1. Choose who can see it. Public display and council sharing are independent choices. You can choose either, both or neither.
  2. Screening before publication. Routine, relevant letters with publication permission may appear after automated screening. Spam, impersonation, threats, abuse, private contact details, sensitive disclosures, personal allegations and unclear authorship or permissions are held for human review. Nothing is automatically forwarded to the council.
  3. Published letters appear below. Publication is not endorsement, fact-checking, a verified signature or a council response.

We apply the same rules to supportive and critical views. A parent's ordinary mention of their child's first name does not by itself prevent publication. Only whitespace and formatting may be changed without asking you. Letters are checked periodically; there is no guaranteed publication or review time.
