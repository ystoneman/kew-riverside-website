## Paid-ads permission (v3, 26 September 2026)

- Google upload: `2026-09-26-videos-v3`. Dropbox permission record: `2026-09-26-videos-dropbox-v3`. The v3 forms keep the `[YouTube v2]` and `[Media v2]` choices unchanged and add one optional, unchecked, not-required checkbox whose answer includes `[Ads v3]`. Missing/blank means no.
- Scope: Yann's own paid ads on YouTube (Google Ads), Facebook and Instagram using the video, or captioned excerpts preserving meaning, with the chosen credit. Purposes: inviting families to learn about or visit the school, and encouraging people to take part in decisions about its future. Not fundraising or donation appeals. The PTA, school or another organisation needs its own permission.
- Operating rules: restrict placements to those three platforms (exclude Messenger, Threads, Audience Network and Google video partners); show the contributor the exact excerpt and ad wording and wait for their agreement before an ad first runs; moderate public comments; send recruitment ads to `index.html#visit-school` or the school's own enquiry route; stop all ads by 30 September 2028.
- Withdrawal: pause or stop ads using the video as soon as possible, normally within two working days; start no new ones. Withdrawing YouTube permission also ends YouTube ads. Platform ad-library records (up to seven years for political/social-issue ads) and others' copies may remain.
- Earlier records (v1, v2) grant no paid-ads permission, whatever else they include. To add it, Yann sends the full current paid-ads wording and privately records the dated reply against the matching recording. Ask at most once; do not chase.
