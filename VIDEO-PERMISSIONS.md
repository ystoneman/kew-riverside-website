## New notices

- Dropbox permission record: `2026-09-22-videos-dropbox-v2`.
- Required: adult/self-recording attestation, receipt/storage/personal-review consent and explicit YouTube publication checkbox. YouTube answer includes `[YouTube v2]`.
- Optional, unchecked: separate news-media checkbox, answer includes `[Media v2]`. Missing/blank means no permission. This permits Yann to supply video/chosen credit and the recording/excerpts to be used in broadcast/online coverage of Kew Riverside’s future, with accurate captions and meaning-preserving edits. Private contact details are excluded. Recipient organisations remain responsible for their own privacy/release arrangements.
- Unmatched or ambiguous uploads remain private. Never infer consent from the upload itself, a current form schema, or a new policy version.
- Keep minimal dated permission/version, disclosure and withdrawal records privately. Before any publication or media disclosure, check matching recording, chosen credit and current withdrawal status. Media withdrawal stops further supply; contact known recipients about the request without promising recall. YouTube withdrawal removes Yann’s upload. Handle storage/review withdrawal and deletion manually under the notice.
