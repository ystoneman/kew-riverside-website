Privacy & moderation · Updated 22 September 2026

Your words.
Your choice to share.

This parent-led website is operated by Yann Stoneman, a Kew Riverside parent who wants to help keep the school open. He is responsible for the information collected through it. It is not operated by Kew Riverside Primary School, Richmond Council, the PTA or an agreed parent group.

Public-role information

To explain who is responsible for the school decision, the public responsibilities section identifies the committee’s current chair and vice-chair using their official council names and roles, source links and a checked date. Our basis is our legitimate interest in explaining a public decision accurately. We have assessed the necessity of these limited fields and the balance with the individuals’ rights. Inclusion establishes no view on closure.

These entries are public and may be indexed or copied. We do not reproduce personal contact details, unrelated personal information, party affiliation or portraits. No personal political opinion or individual vote is published in this first version. We check current offices against official sources and review entries after relevant meetings. Historical roles will be retained only where needed to explain a dated record; inaccurate current information should be corrected.

You can request a correction, object or raise a privacy concern privately. That form has no publication option, works without JavaScript and sends requests for human review. We consider each objection and the grounds for continued processing; public availability does not by itself settle that assessment. The service providers, retention rules and data rights below apply to these requests too. This section was added on 21 September 2026; the existing form consent choices are unchanged.

What we collect and why

The ideas, evidence and questions form collects your message, category, any public source link, optional display name, optional reply email, publication preference, notice version and a reference number. Formspree also processes technical information needed to receive and protect submissions, such as network and browser information.

We use these submissions to improve the website, assess public evidence and corrections, consider questions for the school meeting, answer requests, assess possible community funding and prevent abuse. Our basis for this processing is our legitimate interest in maintaining a useful, accurate and safe parent-led information site. Publication of your message and chosen display name on the suggestions board is optional and based on your consent. We handle data rights requests to meet our legal obligations. We do not use feedback for marketing.

Please do not send children's details, health or SEND information, private school records, photographs, allegations or other sensitive information. Use this form for practical ideas, public evidence and questions. To share your personal views about the school’s future, use community letters, which have the separate purposes and consent choices below. The funding category is for assessing feasibility. Evidence and meeting questions receive human assessment; nothing is automatically sent to the council or meeting, and we cannot guarantee inclusion. Neither form is an urgent or official consultation channel. Ideas notice: 2026-09-22-v3; earlier submissions keep their original permissions.

Crowdfunding and funding ideas

The funding category collects your idea or concern, any public source you choose to provide, optional reply email, category, notice version and private reference. Its purpose is to assess whether community funding could support a viable recovery plan, and to follow up about your contribution if you leave an email. We rely on our legitimate interest in assessing options and coordinating this parent-led initiative. Please keep political opinions, sensitive personal information, payment details and other people’s donor identities or private contact details out of this form; community letters have separate choices for expressing your views.

Funding responses stay private and receive human review, with OpenAI assistance where needed. The display-name and publication choices do not apply to this category, including when JavaScript is unavailable or publication permission is supplied. Nothing is automatically published, forwarded to the council or a third party, added to a mailing list, or treated as a pledge or commitment to help. We would need separate permission before quoting or attributing a private response. Verified public sources can inform editorial updates.

No campaign or pledge collection is being launched. We review funding messages and follow-up copies for deletion within 90 days of receipt, sooner when no longer needed or following a valid request. An active enquiry or dispute may require a recorded longer period. The service providers and manual retention limits below apply. Request access, correction or deletion, or object to processing.

Named supporters

The supporter form is separate from ordinary feedback and community letters. It records your chosen public name, private confirmation email, the exact keep-open statement and version, age/self-submission declaration, processing and publication consents, notice version and private reference. It is for adults submitting their own support, not names collected on behalf of other people or organisations.

We rely on your consent to process the request and your separate explicit consent to publish your chosen name with the exact statement. Both include any political opinion the endorsement may reveal. Consent to an ordinary feedback message, a letter or council sharing cannot replace supporter consent. You can use the rest of the site and contribute in other ways without joining the list.

Yann uses the email to confirm the proposed name and statement with you. A reply confirming those details and human approval are required before publication. This confirms the contributor’s choice, not their legal identity. The confirmation email, consent evidence and private reference stay private. Only the chosen public name, public reference, listing date and confirmation/review label appear with the statement on the website. Nothing is automatically sent to the council, published by AI or used as a mailing-list or volunteer-register signup.

Public entries can be indexed, copied and retained in GitHub’s history. Withdraw consent or request correction/removal privately, giving the public entry link or enough detail to locate the request. We review unpublished requests and confirmation correspondence for deletion within 90 days of receipt, sooner on withdrawal or when no longer needed. Minimal consent and confirmation evidence is kept while the entry remains published; an active request or dispute may require a documented longer period. Formspree notifications and review copies need separate manual attention. Supporter notice: 2026-09-21-supporters-v1.

Private messages to Yann

The About page’s contact form collects your message, optional reply email, consent and notice version. Its purpose is a private conversation about this initiative, existing organising efforts or an offer of help. We rely on your explicit consent to store and review the message, including any political opinions you choose to express, using Formspree and OpenAI, and reply about the request. There is no publication option, automatic forwarding, newsletter signup or volunteer register. You can withdraw consent privately. We review these messages and follow-up copies for deletion within 90 days of receipt, sooner when no longer needed or following withdrawal; longer retention for an active request or dispute must be recorded. Contact notice: 2026-09-21-contact-v1.

Community letters and council sharing

The community letter form is for adults submitting their own words about Kew Riverside's future. It collects the letter, optional display name, optional reply email, notice version, private reference and the choices you make. We use your explicit consent to store and screen a letter using Formspree and OpenAI, including automated screening and any political opinions you choose to express. We do not ask for party affiliation, voting intention or information about other people's health, beliefs or private lives. Please keep that information out of letters.

You make two further, separate choices: whether we may publish your letter and display name after automated screening or human review, and whether we may include your letter in a collection shared with the London Borough of Richmond upon Thames Council about the school's future. Both choices start unchecked. A public letter can use a nickname or Anonymous. Agreeing to one choice does not imply agreement to the other.

If you choose council sharing, you may also provide a private full name and postcode to give the council context. Neither is mandatory or verified. They are never copied from your display name, never included in the public website or its Git history, and are used only to prepare and share the consented council collection. Your reply email is not included. Details sent without council permission are not used for this purpose and are flagged for deletion review.

Routine, relevant letters with publication permission may be published after automated screening without a person reviewing them first. We hold spam, impersonation, threats, abuse, private contact details, sensitive information about children or others, personal allegations, and unclear authorship or permissions for human review. Supportive and critical opinions follow the same rules; an unfamiliar viewpoint or awkward English is not evidence of spam. A parent mentioning their child’s first name in an ordinary testimonial is not by itself a reason to hold it. Letters are checked periodically and publication depends on screening and the website update, with no guaranteed time. Submissions under earlier notices retain their original permissions and require human review before publication. Letter notice: 2026-09-22-letters-v3.

If you supply a reply email and your letter is published, we send one publication email only after confirming it is live. It includes the public link and displayed name and invites you to reply for edits or removal at any time. We may also reply about your letter. This does not add you to a mailing list. You can also use the private request form without an email. Substantive changes to your letter need your agreement.

There is no automatic forwarding to the council. Before a collection is sent, the operator must verify the receiving route, the council's handling requirements, current permissions and any withdrawals. We do not guarantee sending, acceptance or counting as a consultation response. You should respond directly through the official consultation route as well.

Once the council receives information, it handles it under its own privacy, retention and disclosure rules. We cannot guarantee confidentiality at the council or recall information already lawfully used by it. Before sending, we will assess any publication or disclosure conditions against the permission given here and seek further permission if necessary.

Parent Voices video submissions

The video submission route is for adults uploading their own recording, with no other identifiable people in the picture or background audio. For the Dropbox route, we collect your video, filename and upload information, the name and email entered at upload, email entered in the permission form, optional public credit, submission timestamps and your permission choices. No Google or Dropbox account is required. Use the same email in both steps so Yann can match them. The filename question is optional if your phone does not show it. Yann Stoneman is responsible for this parent-led collection. It is separate from the school, PTA and council consultation.

Videos go to Yann’s private Dropbox file-request folder; permission answers stay in his private Google Forms. Other contributors cannot see them. An upload without matching permission stays private and is never treated as permission to publish. Yann personally reviews videos and uses your email for permission checks, replies and any proposed substantive edits. This video route does not use Formspree for uploads or OpenAI for video screening. An AI browser assistant may check submission counts and upload listings to alert Yann; it does not watch videos or assess their content. Listing metadata may be visible during these checks. There is no automatic publication or forwarding to the council.

Our basis is your explicit consent for private receipt, storage and review, including any political opinions you choose to express. Publication requires a separate explicit Yes choice covering your video and chosen credit on Kew Riverside Parent Voices on YouTube. No choice is preselected. You can choose private review only, and you can use the rest of the website or write a letter without uploading a video. Please exclude children’s details, health or SEND information, private contact details, other people’s private information and allegations about identifiable people.

Publication permission includes trimming pauses and adding accurate captions without changing your meaning. Other substantive edits need your agreement. The optional public credit is separate from your private contact and upload details; a nickname or no written credit is fine, but your face and voice may still identify you. Permission does not extend to other social platforms, a council collection, advertising or a mailing list. Publication is discretionary and has no guaranteed date.

Public YouTube videos and credits may be viewed, indexed, downloaded or copied by others. You can withdraw either permission or request access, correction or removal privately. Identify the video using its public link, submission date or filename, and say what you want changed. Yann can remove his upload; he cannot recall copies made by other people. Withdrawal does not change the lawfulness of earlier consented use. The private request form uses Formspree and may be opened by the AI assistant before human handling, as described below; do not attach the video or include sensitive detail in that request.

Yann reviews raw uploads and unpublished submissions for deletion within 90 days of receipt, sooner after withdrawal or when no longer needed. Minimal permission records are kept while a video remains published; an active request or dispute may require a documented longer period. Downloaded editing copies, Dropbox and Google Drive deleted-file areas, permission records and correspondence need separate manual attention. This is a manual retention process, not an automatic deletion service. Public videos remain while relevant and permitted.

Keep recordings short and ideally under 100 MB. Available storage is limited and intake may stop when it is full. File requests do not establish identity or prove consent, and may receive unwanted or unmatched files. Contact Yann privately for an alternative if a service is unavailable. Dropbox and Google handle service and technical data under their own terms; see Dropbox’s privacy policy, Google’s privacy policy and Google’s international transfer information. These services are contacted only when you follow their links; no upload form or video player is embedded here. Dropbox route notice: 2026-09-22-videos-dropbox-v1.

Original Google upload form: the previous form remains open, including for people with an older flyer. It requires Google sign-in and stores its videos and responses in Yann’s private Google Drive and Forms. Google records the account name, email and profile photo associated with the upload. It accepts one video up to 1 GB, one response per account, subject to the form’s storage cap. Its original notice and consent choices (2026-09-22-videos-v1) still apply; moving to Dropbox does not expand permission for earlier submissions. You do not need to resubmit a completed Google upload. The same separate YouTube choice, manual review, withdrawal and retention rules above apply.

Where written submissions go

Written submissions go to a private Formspree dashboard and the operator's notification inbox. They do not appear on the website immediately. The operator uses OpenAI through Codex for automated letter screening and to assist with reviewing other messages. Reply emails and private council identity fields are not needed for content moderation. We avoid including them in review excerpts where the dashboard allows; opening a submission may expose those fields to the AI browser assistant. Do not treat these services as a confidential channel for sensitive records. Messages themselves may be processed by OpenAI, so please keep personal information out of the message.

Formspree hosts its service in the United States and says it uses Standard Contractual Clauses as a processor. Providers may process data outside the UK. See Formspree's privacy policy, its security information and OpenAI's privacy policy for their data practices and transfer information.

What can become public

Only eligible ideas and suggestions whose authors choose the optional publication permission can be considered for the suggestions board. Funding ideas and privacy/removal requests are excluded regardless of any publication permission supplied. Named supporters follow the separate consent and confirmation process above. Routine website suggestions may be approved after AI review when the planned hourly service is active. It is currently paused. Community letters follow the separate publication choices and screening rules above. Named supporters always need human approval. Private contact, correction and privacy requests are never automatically published. Evidence, meeting questions, corrections and submissions with source links require human assessment. For the suggestions board, we hold sensitive, uncertain or factual matters for a person. Constructive criticism is welcome and is assessed using the same rules as positive feedback.

Published items contain the approved text, your display name (or Anonymous), publication date, a public reference and a review label; website suggestions also have a status. Letters are labelled AI screened or Human reviewed according to the assessment they received. We do not publish reply emails, private council name/postcode fields or private submission references. Display names and identities are not verified. Letters are opinions, not endorsements, verified signatures or a representative poll. Approval means the suggestion is suitable to display; it does not verify factual claims or commit the operator to implement it.

The board is hosted publicly on GitHub Pages. Published text and display names may be indexed, copied or retained in GitHub's version history. We can remove it from the live board, but cannot recall copies made by other people. Please only permit publication of information you are comfortable sharing publicly.

Retention

The current Formspree plan provides 30 days of dashboard submission history. Notification emails and AI review conversations are separate copies and are not automatically deleted when that history expires. We keep them only while needed to handle the feedback, follow-up or a dispute; you can request their deletion. Minimal private moderation records help prevent duplicate publication. For letters, the operator reviews unpublished letters and private council details for deletion within 90 days of receipt, or sooner after withdrawal or when no longer needed. An active request or dispute can require longer retention, which must be recorded. Consent evidence is retained while the relevant use continues. Notification emails and AI review copies need separate attention; this is a manual retention process, not automatic deletion by Formspree. Published items remain while relevant, unless removed following review or a request.

Your choices and requests

You can send feedback or a letter without a name or email (the Dropbox video route needs an email address but no account), leave either sharing choice unchecked, withdraw consent for letter processing, publication or council sharing, object to processing based on legitimate interests, request access, correction or deletion, or ask for a person to review an automated moderation decision. Other rights, including restriction and portability, may apply depending on the circumstances.

Send a private privacy, removal or human-review request. Say which permission you want to withdraw. Include your private reference if you kept it, or the public item link and enough detail to locate it. Add an email only if you want a reply. These requests are held for the operator and never published automatically. We may need information to verify a request before disclosing or changing data.

If you are unhappy with how your information is handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Cookies and spam protection

The evidence hub does not run analytics or advertising scripts. The feedback page does not load a CAPTCHA script while you browse. When you send feedback, you continue to Formspree, which may use Google's reCAPTCHA and associated cookies or technical signals to protect the submission. See Google's privacy policy. Formspree also applies its spam filter and service rate limits. No method blocks every abusive submission.

Letters are checked periodically under the screening rules above. The separate planned hourly review of website suggestions is not yet active. There is no guaranteed response or publication time for ordinary suggestions or letters. Statutory data-rights requests are handled within applicable deadlines. Use official channels for time-sensitive, school or safeguarding matters.
