Privacy & moderation · 21 September 2026

Your feedback.
Your choice to share.

This independent evidence hub is operated by Yann Stoneman, who is responsible for the feedback collected through it. It is not operated by Kew Riverside Primary School, Richmond Council or a campaign group.

What we collect and why

The feedback form collects your message, category, any public source link, optional reply email, publication preference, notice version and a reference number. Formspree also processes technical information needed to receive and protect submissions, such as network and browser information.

We use feedback to improve the website, assess corrections, answer requests and prevent abuse. Our basis for this processing is our legitimate interest in maintaining a useful, accurate and safe community information site. Publication on the suggestions board is optional and based on your permission. We handle data rights requests to meet our legal obligations. We do not use feedback for marketing.

Please do not send children's details, health or SEND information, private school records, photographs, allegations or other sensitive information. This form is for website feedback, not urgent matters or official consultation responses.

Where submissions go

Submissions go to a private Formspree dashboard and the operator's notification inbox. They do not appear on the website immediately. The operator uses OpenAI through Codex to assist with reviewing messages. Optional email addresses are not needed for moderation and are excluded from AI review where the dashboard allows. Messages themselves may be processed by OpenAI, so please keep personal information out of the message.

Formspree hosts its service in the United States and says it uses Standard Contractual Clauses as a processor. Providers may process data outside the UK. See Formspree's privacy policy, its security information and OpenAI's privacy policy for their data practices and transfer information.

What can become public

Only messages whose authors choose the optional publication permission can be considered for the public board. Routine website suggestions may be approved automatically after AI review. We hold sensitive, uncertain or factual matters for a person. Constructive criticism is welcome and is assessed using the same rules as positive feedback.

Published suggestions contain the approved text, a publication date, a public reference, a review label and a status. We do not publish reply emails or private submission references. Approval means the suggestion is suitable to display; it does not verify factual claims or commit the operator to implement it.

The board is hosted publicly on GitHub Pages. Published text may be indexed, copied or retained in GitHub's version history. We can remove it from the live board, but cannot recall copies made by other people. Please only permit publication of information you are comfortable sharing publicly.

Retention

The current Formspree plan provides 30 days of dashboard submission history. Notification emails and AI review conversations are separate copies and are not automatically deleted when that history expires. We keep them only while needed to handle the feedback, follow-up or a dispute; you can request their deletion. Minimal private moderation records help prevent duplicate publication. Approved suggestions remain on the board while relevant, unless removed following review or a request.

Your choices and requests

You can send feedback without a name or email, leave publication permission unchecked, withdraw that permission, object to processing based on legitimate interests, request access, correction or deletion, or ask for a person to review an automated moderation decision. Other rights, including restriction and portability, may apply depending on the circumstances.

Send a private privacy, removal or human-review request. Include your private reference if you kept it, or the public suggestion link and enough detail to locate it. Add an email only if you want a reply. These requests are held for the operator and never published automatically. We may need information to verify a request before disclosing or changing data.

If you are unhappy with how your information is handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Cookies and spam protection

The evidence hub does not run analytics or advertising scripts. The feedback page does not load a CAPTCHA script while you browse. When you send feedback, you continue to Formspree, which may use Google's reCAPTCHA and associated cookies or technical signals to protect the submission. See Google's privacy policy. Formspree also applies its spam filter and service rate limits. No method blocks every abusive submission.

Review is planned approximately hourly when the reviewer is available. There is no guaranteed response time. Use official channels for time-sensitive, school or safeguarding matters.
