Interest form privacy · 25 September 2026

Your details stay private

Yann Stoneman is responsible for this form in a personal capacity. It is not operated by the school, council, PTA or an agreed parent group.

The form asks for optional adult first name and email, interest in each date, estimated household numbers and optional practical help. Please do not include children’s names, classes, health details or personal stories. Leaving contact details blank means checking this page yourself for changes; Google may still process technical information, so the form is not promised to be anonymous.

Yann uses responses only to estimate attendance, plan practical help and, if you provide an email, send practical updates about the selected gatherings or contact you about your offer of help. The basis is your consent, including explicit consent for any political opinions your response may reveal. The required consent choice is separate from reading this page or attending. There is no newsletter signup, public guest list, or photography permission.

Responses are held privately in Google Forms, accessible only to the form owner. No responses are published on this website or stored in its public code repository. Google provides the form service and may process information outside the UK; see Google’s privacy policy and international data-transfer information.

Yann will delete identifiable form responses by 12 December 2026, sooner if no longer needed or if you withdraw consent. This is a manual deletion commitment; Google’s own retention and backup practices also apply. Any later campaign updates or new use need a separate choice.

You can withdraw consent or request access, correction or deletion through the private privacy request form. Say it concerns the gatherings form and give enough information to find your response; completely unidentifiable responses may be impossible to match. Withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful use. Other rights can apply; you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office. The private request form has its own website privacy notice.
