Private correction or objection

Help keep the
public record accurate.

Report a factual error, raise a privacy concern or object to the use of public-role information. This form sends a private request to the site operator for human review. Requests are never published on the suggestions board.

10–3,000 characters. Identify the page or entry, explain the concern and the change you request. Include a document date where relevant. Please do not include children’s identities or unnecessary private information.

Your email stays private. Without it, we cannot send you a direct reply.

Formspree receives your request for the site operator. OpenAI may assist review, but a person handles corrections and objections. Read how your information is used.

You’ll continue to Formspree for a security check and confirmation. Delivery is not confirmed if it shows an error. Keep a copy and the date for follow-up.

Private by design.

There is no publication option on this form. The request enters the privacy and human-review queue, including when JavaScript is disabled.

The operator checks the relevant source and context before changing an entry. An objection is assessed on its circumstances. No allegation about a person is automatically published.

Review is manual. We aim to handle concerns promptly; statutory data-rights requests are handled within applicable deadlines. This is not a school complaint, safeguarding or official consultation channel.
